# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want participation in this project to be safe and respectful. Treat people
with patience, assume good intent, and focus criticism on the work.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, and deliberate
disruption are not acceptable. Do not publish someone else's private
information without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, code, issues, and
other contributions that violate this standard. They may temporarily or
permanently ban a contributor for behavior they consider inappropriate.

Report problems privately to uchihaabdellahi@gmail.com. Reports will be
reviewed promptly and kept confidential as far as reasonably possible.
